Know some details about

AFFIDAVIT 1946
FEATURES
What must be reported?

Who is required to submit it?
Any person with residence in Chile who
remits or pays income from Chilean
sources derived from transactions carried
out by a foreign person in the country.

Income from investments and
transactions carried out in Chile,
regardless of whether they are
subject to additional tax.

When is it submitted?

How is it submitted?

You must submit your statement by the
last business day of June each year,
covering all transactions from the
previous year.

Through the Servicio de Impuestos Internos
website: sii.cl > Online services > Affidavits >
Income tax Affidavits.

DECLARE AND AVOID SANCTIONS
Delay or omission in submission

Partial or erroneous submission

Malicious submission of incomplete or false information

A fine ranging from 1 UTM* to 1
UTA**, according to No. 1 of Article 97
of the Tax Code.

Fine no less than 1% or more than
100% of 1 UTA, according to Article
109 of the Tax Code.

Ordinary imprisonment in its medium to
maximum terms and a fine up to 300%
of the amount defrauded, according to
No. 4 of Article 97 of the Tax Code.

Referential values: UTM June 2023: $63,236 / UTA June 2023: $759,156

More information at sii.cl

*UTM: Monthly Tax Unit **UTA: Annual Tax Unit

on transactions in Chile
