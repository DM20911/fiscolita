Affidavits 1937 Country-by-Country Report and 1950 Master File
FEATURES
Who is required to submit these Affidavits?

A

The parent or controlling entity of the Multinational
Enterprise Group (MNE) with residence in Chile, if the
total revenues are at least 750 million euros at the
time of closing the consolidated financial statements,
based on the exchange rate observed as of 1st January
20151, determined by the Central Bank of Chile.

B

The entity that is part of or belongs to the MNE, with
residency in Chile for tax purposes, and has been
designated as the sole substitute for the Parent or
Controlling Entity to submit the Affidavit “Country-byCountry Report” in its tax residence country on behalf of
the MNE.

1. 738.05 Chilean pesos per Euro.
