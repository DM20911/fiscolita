Know some details about

AFFIDAVIT 1929
on transactions abroad

FEATURES
Who is required to submit it?

What must be reported?
Investments made abroad, regardless of
whether they generated income during the
period, and transactions generating income
from foreign sources, regardless of
whether they were taxed abroad or
remitted to the country.

Any person with residence in Chile who
conducts investments abroad or receives
income from abroad.

When is it submitted?

How is it submitted?

You must submit your statement by the
last business day of June each year,
covering all transactions and investments
conducted abroad during the preceding
fiscal year.

These statements are submitted through the
Servicio de Impuestos Internos website:
sii.cl > Online services > Affidavits > Income
tax Affidavits.

DECLARE AND AVOID SANCTIONS
FROM 10 UTA TO 100 UTA
First-category taxpayers with
investments abroad that are not
reported

FROM 10 UTM TO 100 UTM
Taxpayers conducting investments
abroad eligible for foreign tax credits
that are not reported.

FROM 10 UTA TO 50 UTA

FROM 1 UTM TO 1 UTA

Taxpayers controlling foreign
entities who fail to report their
transactions.

Taxpayers who omit or submit late statements for
transactions other than investments. If the Form is
incomplete or incorrect, the fine will range from
1% to 100% of 1 UTA.

If two or more fines are incurred, according to the principle of speciality, only the most severe sanction will apply.
