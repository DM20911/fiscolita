Tutorial to Report a noncompliant business
Here we describe the mechanism that the SII has enabled in the Digital VAT Portal to file
complaints referred to:
•

Breach of the obligation to register in the Simplified Tax Regime, established in article 35 A of
the VAT Law, by foreign providers and sellers of goods without domicile or residence in Chile.

•

Declaration, by foreign providers and sellers of goods without domicile or residence in Chile, of a
tax base lower than the one that legally corresponds, or any other breach of legal
regulations or instructions issued by the SII regarding the declaration and payment of the VAT
levied on services and the sale of goods.

Steps to follow
1. Select the option "Contact" available in the upper right bar of the Digital VAT Portal.

2. In this option, two alternatives are displayed, depending on whether you are registered in the
Simplified Regime or not; you must select the corresponding option.

3. Complete the required information on the form. Your name and email address will allow us to
contact you if necessary.

4. Select the "Report a noncompliant business" option from the "Subject" list if your company is a
registered taxpayer, or choose “Other” if your company is not a registered taxpayer.
5. Enter all the information associated with your complaint in the "Message" field. By completing
the required fields, the form will be sent.
6. Send Form
Upon completion, your complaint will be sent to our systems.

To facilitate the evaluation and analysis of your complaint, we remind you to report the name of
the noncompliant business and all the information that you can consider relevant. If we need
more information, we will contact you.
