## Frequently Asked Questions

## 3.- Regarding the filing and payment of the VAT for remote services and remote sales performed by taxpayers without domicile or residence in Chile.

#### [1.- How is VAT declared?](#collapseT1)

VAT is declared exclusively online through the Digital VAT Portal that the Internal Revenue Service has enabled on its website (sii.cl).

The declaration
must be made using the Digital VAT Form (F129). For declarations in foreign
currency, payments must be made using a SWIFT transfer (international interbank
transfer).

To fulfill the obligation, it is not enough to just send the tax
return. The tax must be paid within the legal term.

#### [2.- How to determine the taxable base?](#collapseT2)

The taxable base for VAT purposes corresponds to the total amount of payments received, whatever its nature, for both the provision of taxed services and/or the sales of taxed goods.

For instance, the tax base in the case of intermediation services would be the commission charged by the respective provider, or any other income received. On the other hand, the tax base associated to the provision of digital content such as videos, music, games or others, would be the value paid by the consumer, which could correspond to a price or rate.

In the case of goods, the tax base corresponds to the total price of the good, including any incidental charge.

#### [3.- What information should be entered in the "Rebates" field?](#collapseT3)

This field must be only used to register VAT corresponding to discounts, cancellations or terminations of services or sales, provided that it has been refunded to the consumer. If applicable, this amount will be automatically deducted from the tax payable.

As an example: a consumer claims against his streaming service provider for services charged, but not received, in the amount of US $ 20 (net of tax). The service provider returns the $ 20 to the consumer. Therefore, the amount to be recorded by the provider in the Rebates field will be US $ 3.8 (US $ 20 \* 19%).

#### [4.- How is the tax to pay determined?](#collapseT4)

The tax amount is the result of applying a 19% on the tax base. This calculation is automatically done by the system once a F129 return is made.

#### [5.- When should I file and pay a Digital VAT Form (F129)?](#collapseT5)

The filing and payment terms of the F129 depend on the periodicity the taxpayer
chooses at the time of obtaining its User Number through the Digital VAT Portal.
The periodicity refers to the tax period to declare, where “tax period” is
understood as a period of one or three months in a row.

If a monthly tax period
is chosen, filing and payment of the F129 must be made during the first twenty
days of the month following the tax period that is filed. For instance, if
the remote services provided during March of a given year were being declared,
the term for filing and paying is from the first to the twentieth day of April
of the same year.

Now, if a quarterly tax period is chosen, filing and payment
of the F129 must be made during the first twenty days of the month following
the tax period that is declared. For example, if the remote services provided
during April, May and June of a given year were being declared, the term to
file and pay is from the first to the twentieth day of July of the same year.

#### [6.- Is it possible to resume the process of filling out the F129 form, if it had not been sent yet?](#collapseT6)

It is not possible to resume the entry of information in a F129 form since the entered data will only be available in the case that the return had been sent.

#### [7.- Do you have to file the F129 even when you do not have income from services or sales to declare?](#collapseT7)

F129 must be filled only if income from services or sales has been received in the respective tax period.

#### [8.- Can I get a certificate or a copy of the filed F129 form?](#collapseT8)

The system automatically displays a filing certificate when it is filed. Now, if you want to obtain a certificate or a copy of the filing of the F129 form, you must enter the Portal “Check Status” menu. In this menu it is possible to consult the current status of tax returns, by tax period, and obtain filing certificates of an F129 form for the period selected.

#### [9.- If a tax return was filed in a period for a higher amount of the tax that actually corresponded, is it possible to correct the tax return in the same period?](#collapseT9)

You cannot amend your tax return automatically. You will have to submit a request to [ivasd.chile@sii.cl](mailto:ivasd.chile@sii.cl).

#### [10.- Where should the remaining VAT from the previous month be recorded?](#collapseT10)

If there is a VAT remmant of a tax return from a previous period, this amount will be automatically displayed in “VAT Remnants from previous periods " (code 201) of the F129 form to file.

#### [11.- How is the payment of this tax return made?](#collapseT11)

Payments for declarations in foreign currency must be made through a SWIFT
("Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication")
transfer using the data that is automatically displayed when sending the tax
return. Every time an F129 is sent, the data will be displayed to make the
payment. It is very important to correctly complete the information required
to successfully carry out the SWIFT transfer, otherwise the payment cannot
be identified and the filed tax period will be recorded as debt.

Transfers
via SWIFT correspond to an international interbank transfer which are identified
by a SWIFT code, also called the BIC ("Bank Identifier
Code") consisting of an 8 or 11 digit alphanumeric code that is used
to identify the receiving bank when making an international transfer.

Payments
in the case of declarations in Chilean pesos are made online after an F129
has been submitted.

#### [12.- What information should be recorded in SWIFT codes 70 and 71-A?](#collapseT12)

In code 70, the User Number must be registered, followed by the name of the form, tax period and Folio. For example:   
556545253-F129-202006-853201533. This identifier is automatically shown in the informative window that is displayed once the declaration has been sent in order to proceed with the payment via SWIFT transfer.

In code 71-A, the word “OUR” (in capital letters) must be registered, which indicates that the Bank will pay in full to the General Treasury of the Republic of Chile.

It is important that this information is recorded every time a tax return is filed, otherwise it will not be possible to correctly identify the issuer of the payment and the filed period will be recorded as debt.

Note that is important to send the transfer with enough time to allow the payment to be processed within the legal deadline.

#### [13.- In what currency can the F129 be paid?](#collapseT13)

Payments can be made in US dollars, euros or Chilean pesos. The currency to be used must be selected at the time of obtaining the User Number through the Registration Form. It is important to consider that once the payment currency has been selected, changes to this parameter can only be requested between January 21 and January 31 of a given year.

#### [14.- What information can I review in the Check Status?](#collapseT14)

In the Check Status option you can review the status of the tax returns (F129) by tax period.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| No current Tax Return | No return has been submitted for this tax period. |
| Tax Return in force without payment | There is a tax return in force in the period consulted, but there is no payment associated with this return. |
| Tax Return in force with partial payment | There is a tax return in force in the period consulted, but there is no full payment associated with this return. |
| Tax Return in force with full payment | There is a tax return in force in the period consulted, with full payment associated with this return. |

The Check Status option shows, additionally, the following options:

* Information on submitted Form 129.
* Certificate of filing of Form 129.
* Debt consultation. \*
* Information to make payments through a Swift transfer\*\*

**\* Debt information appears only if there is debt.  
 \*\* The information to make the Swift transfer appears only if the tax return payment has not been received or was received incompletely.**

#### [15.- How can you amend the information of a tax return already sent?](#collapseT15)

Taxpayers that, due to errors in the determination of the taxable base or due to improper deductions, submit an F129 with a lower taxable base than the one that effectively corresponds, must file a new return and pay any tax difference. To do this, taxpayers must go to Tax Return/Check status options and select the respective tax period. It should be noted that, if the amendment is made after the deadline, penalties and interest will be applied.

It is important to note that you cannot amend a tax return for an amount less than previously declared. For more information, inquiries can be sent to [ivasd.chile@sii.cl](mailto:ivasd.chile@sii.cl).

#### [16.- In what situations could debt be generated?](#collapseT17)

Debt can be generated in the following situations:

1. Failure to file the tax return for a tax period when there is an obligation within the period prescribed by law.
2. Failure to pay a tax return filed, or by paying a lower amount than reported, within the period established by law.
3. Amend a tax return outside the period established by law.

The legal term to which the preceding numerals refer, corresponds to the 20th of the month following that in which the tax is accrued.

#### [17.- Where can you check the status of debts?](#collapseT18)

It can be done in the same SWIFT transfer of your declaration of the F129. For this, the information of the balance due will appear in the code [300] of the form and will be added to the total amount to be paid.

When presenting debt, the information data to carry out the SWIFT transfer will show a breakdown of the amount to be paid, differentiating the tax payable for the period and the total balance owed.

#### [18.- How can the debt payment be made?](#collapseT19)

It can be done in the same SWIFT transfer of your declaration of the F129. For this, the information of the balance due will appear in the code [300] of the form and will be added to the total amount to be paid.

When presenting debt, the information data to carry out the SWIFT transfer will show a breakdown of the amount to be paid, differentiating the tax payable for the period and the total balance owed.
