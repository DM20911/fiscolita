Digital VAT Form (F129): Filling Instructions
1. The declaration and payment must be made exclusively through the Digital VAT Portal
available at sii.cl.
2. Tax payments in foreign currency must be made through an international interbank transfer,
identified with a SWIFT code, also called the BIC code, consisting of an eight (8) or eleven
(11) digit alphanumeric series.
3. Values in dollars or euros can include up to 2 decimal places.
4. Tax payments in Chilean pesos are made online once the return has been filed (decimals are
not allowed).
5. Before submitting the form, the operations and information registered must be reviewed. If it
contains errors, the statement could be objected.
6. All recorded values must be positive.
7. It is not possible to submit an F129 with blank information or missing information.

Header information
All the fields in this section are automatically displayed and are not editable. They show what
the declarant reported in their registration form:
a) Tax Period (Code 15): The tax period being declared is automatically displayed.
b) User Number (Code 03): The User Number that identifies the taxpayer who is declaring
is automatically displayed.
c) Folio Number (Code 07): The folio number is automatically generated once the
declaration has been sent, it allows the shipment to be identified. Each F129 has a unique,
non-repeatable folio number.

Registration Information
This section contains the following fields: email address, legal name, country of tax residence,
address, website, Tax ID, frequency and currency. These fields are automatically filled using
the declarant's information provided at the time of registration.

Services Provided and/or Sale of Goods
Services provided
a) Amount of transactions (Code 101): In this field must be registered the number of
transactions for which remuneration is received (commissions, fees, sales, income, others)
in the tax period being declared.
b) Taxable base (Code 102): In this field must be registered the amount subject to tax for the
provision of remote services by foreign providers. This amount corresponds to the
remuneration obtained for commissions, sales, rates, income or others.
c) Value Added Tax (Code 103): The amount corresponding to the value added tax is
automatically displayed using the information registered in code 102.

d) Rebates (Code. 104): In this field must be registered the rebates to the VAT due to
discounts, cancellation, rescission or termination of services, provided the taxpayer has
returned to the consumer the tax collected in excess.
Sale of Goods
a) Number of transactions (Code 106): In this field must be registered the number of
transactions that took place in the tax period being declared.
b) Taxable base (Code 107): In this field must be registered the amount subject to VAT for
the sales of taxed goods by nonresident sellers and digital intermediation platforms.
c) Value added tax (Code 108): The amount corresponding to the value added tax is
automatically displayed using the information registered in code 107.
d) Rebates (Code. 109): In this field must be registered the rebates to VAT due to
discounts, cancellation, rescission or termination of transactions, provided that the taxpayer
has returned to the buyer the tax collected in excess.
Total
a) Number of transactions (Code 117): This field automatically records the sum of the
number of transactions corresponding to codes 101 and 106 for which payment is received
in the tax period being declared.
b) Taxable base (Code 118): This field automatically records the sum of the taxable base
registered in codes 102 and 107 corresponding to the tax period being declared.
c) Value added tax (Code 119): This field automatically records the sum of the value added
tax registered in codes 103 and 108 corresponding to the tax period being declared.
d) Rebates (Code. 120): This field automatically records the sum of rebates registered in
codes 104 and 109 corresponding to the tax period being declared.

VAT Remnants
a) VAT remnants from previous periods (Code 201): In this field is automatically recorded
the remnants of VAT in favor of the taxpayer accumulated in previous periods, which may
be deducted from the VAT to be paid in the tax period being declared.
b) VAT remnants accumulated for subsequent periods (Code 202): In this field is
automatically registered the remnants of VAT in favor of the taxpayer that accumulate for
subsequent periods, which in turn could be deducted from the VAT to be paid in those
subsequent periods.

Total Tax Determined
a. Total tax determined (Code 547): In this field is automatically registered the difference
between codes 119 and 120, provided that code 119 is greater than code 120.
b. Total to be paid in legal terms (Code 91): In this field is automatically registered the
amount determined in Code 547.
c. Adjustments (Code 92): The system will automatically calculate adjustments that could
affect the declaration when an F129 is sent. (Chilean pesos currency only)
d. Fines and interest (Code 93): The system will automatically calculate fines and interest that
could affect the declaration when an F129 is sent.

e. Total to be paid with surcharge (Code 94): The system will automatically calculate the
sum of codes 91 and 93 when sending the form, in the event of fines and interest.

Debt Previous Tax Periods
a. Amount due previous periods (Code 300): In this field is automatically recorded the
debt in force from previous periods.
b. Total to pay (Code 301): In this field is automatically displayed the total tax amount to
be paid, considering the outstanding debts of Code 300, if applicable.
