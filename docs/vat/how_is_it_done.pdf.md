Foreign businesses or platforms without domicile

Provide taxed digital services.

Steps to complete the Registration Form
First you must enter the Digital VAT Portal and select the option “Registration Form” in
the Enrollment and Registration section, as shown in the picture:

Step 1: Registration Information
This section requests information regarding the company/organization that is being
register.

Step 2: Tax and Contact Information
In this section, some tax information about the company/organization is requested, as
well as contact information for communication purposes with the SII.

Step 3: Registration and Enrollment Details
In this section, you must choose the frequency with which the tax return and payment will
be made, and the currency in which the transfers will be made.

NOTE: The periodicity of declaration and currency of payment can be modified between
January 21th and January 31th of each year. If you want to modify these parameters,
you must previously send an email to ivasd.chile@sii.cl.

Step 4: Additional Contact Information
Additionally, information must be entered for two other contacts different from the one
registered in the Tax and Contact Information section.

Step 5: Sworn Statement
When finished, please read the following statement carefully and accept its terms.
Finally, select “Submit Form.”

Step 6: Email verification and User Number assignment
Once the Registration Form has been submitted, the taxpayer will receive an email at
the email address provided in Step 2, Tax and Contact Information. The email will contain
a Verification Code.

Enter the verification code received in the email on the same Portal navigation screen,
or by selecting the “Enter verification code” option on the authentication screen.
NOTE: By entering the verification code, the email will be validated, and a User Number
will be automatically assigned. This number will be sent to the same email address as a
backup in case it is forgotten.

Step 7: Password Creation
When entering the verification code that validates your email, four fields will appear
that you must complete to create your password, as shown in the picture.
NOTE: The Secret answer is case sensitive.
By clicking on "Create password" you will be authenticated on the Portal. Additionally,
an email will be sent to you with the User Number assigned, in case of forgetting.

Tax Return Inquiries
First, you must log in to the Digital VAT Portal and select the button in the Declaration
and Payment section, with the option you wish to inquire:

1.- Check Status:
Through this option, you can check the status of your tax return and payment by
selecting the month and year corresponding to the tax period of the return you wish to
consult.

The following statuses may appear in this query:
• No current tax return: It means that you have not sent a tax return for the period
consulted.
• Current tax return without payment: It means that you sent a tax return for the
period consulted, but it does not record an associated payment.
• Current tax return with partial payment: It means that you sent a tax return for
the period consulted and there is an associated payment, but for a partial
amount of the total to be paid.
• Current tax return paid: It means that you send a tax return and payment for the
period consulted.

NOTE: Remember that the Swift transfer can take up to three business days to be reported to the
Chilean Tax Administration, so it could take a few days for the information to be updated.

2.- Check Debt:
This option allows you to check outstanding debts. Depending on the case, information
will be displayed for all returns that show a debt since registration.
Outstanding debts
