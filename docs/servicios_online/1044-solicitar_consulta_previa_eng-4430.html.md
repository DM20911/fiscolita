## Pre-filing consultation

Download and complete the form to request an advance transfer pricing arrangement pre-filing consultation.

Then send the form to the email [area.apa@sii.cl](mailto:area.apa@sii.cl). Subsequently, you will receive an email with the date (day and time) of the meeting.
