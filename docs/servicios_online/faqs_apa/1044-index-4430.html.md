## Reference Material – Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

[##### General background information](#)

#### [What is an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse1)

An Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) is an arrangement that sets, in advance of transactions between related parties, a set of criteria for the determination of the transfer pricing for those transactions over a fixed period of time.  
  
Specifically in Chile, Number 7 of Article 41 E of the Income Tax Law, contained in Article 1 of Decree-Law No. 824 of 1974, contemplates the possibility for taxpayers engaged in cross-border transactions with related parties abroad to propose an APA to the Servicio de Impuestos Internos, through which the arm's length price, value, or return is determined and agreed in advance for the transactions to be carried out over a determined period of time.

#### [What types of Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) exist?](#collapse2)

The types of Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) that exist are
the following:   
  
a) Unilateral APA: Is the one entered
into by a taxpayer and the tax administration of a country where it is subject
to taxes.   
b) Bilateral APA: Is the one that
involves three parties: the taxpayer (including its related party), the tax
administration of the country of origin, and the tax administration of the
jurisdiction where its related party is subject to tax.   
c) Multilateral APA: Is the arrangement between taxpayers
and the tax administration of their country of residence and more than one
foreign tax administration.   
  
According to the provision of numeral 7 of Article 41 E of the Income Tax
Law, the SII can enter into all the types of arrangements previously described.

#### [Who participates in an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) concerning the import of goods?](#collapse3)

When an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) concerns the import of goods, it
must be signed by the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional
de Aduanas.   
  
For these purposes, the Ministry of Finance established the procedure for
joint action of the Services through Exempt Resolution No. 64 dated 24/03/2016.
Among other topics, the resolution indicates that APAs must be submitted to
the Servicio de Impuestos Internos, which must then inform the Servicio Nacional
de Aduanas. It also establishes that the Services will, through a resolution,
set the coordination instances and their respective procedures and deadlines
for the purposes of resolving the APAs.  
  
In APAs concerning the import of goods,
the Servicio de Impuestos Internos acts as the coordinator of the entire procedure
before the taxpayer, with the Departamento de Análisis de Riesgos Internacionales of the Subdirección de Fiscalización
being responsible for this process.

#### [What are the main objectives of Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA)?](#collapse4)

An Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) is a cooperative procedure
between the tax administration and a taxpayer, and has two main objectives:  

* Saving administrative burden and time for tax administrations.
* Legal certainty regarding the outcomes achieved for taxpayers.

#### [What are the fundamental aspects of an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) according to the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD)?](#collapse5)

* They are initiated at the taxpayer's initiative.
* They involve negotiations between a taxpayer and one or more tax administrations.
* It is preferable for them to have a low degree of specificity and be limited
  to determining the applicable methodology.
* They can be cancelled in case of fraud or misrepresentation of the agreement's
  information.
* The OECD advocates for bilateral or multilateral APAs, which reduce the possibility
  of double economic taxation. In unilateral APAs, it proposes informing the
  authorities of potentially affected jurisdictions.
* It is a cooperative process
  between taxpayers and tax administrations, assuming the intervention of the
  enterprise and a thorough analysis of the provided information.

#### [Where are Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) regulated in Chilean legislation?](#collapse6)

* Article 41 E No. 7 of the Income Tax Law.
* Circular No. 10 dated 30/01/2025
* Exempt Resolution No. 28 dated 06/03/2025
* Exempt Resolution No. 64 dated 24/03/2016, by the Ministry of Finance.
* SII Exempt Resolution No. 54 dated 17/06/2016
* SNA Exempt Resolution No. 3637 dated 17/06/2016

#### [What are the advantages of an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) for the taxpayers and the Servicio de Impuestos Internos?](#collapse7)

a)  Greater supply and exchange of information between
the parties.   
b)  Validation of the indicated valuation methods.   
c)  Validation of the comparables used.   
d)  Economisation of resources, means, and time for the Servicio de Impuestos
Internos.   
e)  Certainty granted to taxpayers once they have been approved (especially
in bilateral and multilateral arrangements). On the other hand, the tax administration
can obtain a certain level of certainty regarding tax collection.   
f)  The absence of transfer pricing adjustments during the validity of
the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) helps to predict the costs and expenses
of enterprises more securely, resulting in better profitability of their investments.

#### [What are the stages of an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse8)

1. Preliminary study and analysis of the proposal, before the formal request
   for the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA), if deemed necessary by the enterprises.
2. Formal request for the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA).
3. Processing.
4. Conclusion (signing of the agreement or rejection of the request).
5. Monitoring
   or follow-up of the signed agreement.

#### [What transactions can an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) cover?](#collapse9)

The Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) can be applied to any international transaction carried out between two or more related enterprises in accordance with Article 41 E of the Income Tax Law. In this sense, it can be applied to the universe of related transactions, one or several transactions, as determined by the taxpayer.

#### [Can the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, enter into an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) in which other tax administrations intervene?](#collapse10)

Yes, the Service(s) can enter into an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) in which other tax administrations intervene.

Moreover, the SII could also require the intervention of other tax administrations when dealing with the extension or renewal of an APA.

#### [What does the first section of numeral 7 of Article 41 E of the Income Tax Law refer to when it indicates that the taxpayer’s representative must be expressly authorised to sign the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse11)

This legal requirement implies that the representative(s) of the taxpayer must have express authority in their mandate or power to enter into advance pricing arrangements.

In accordance with Article 9 of the Tax Code and Circular No. 4 of 2022, when a person acts on behalf of a taxpayer, they must have the necessary authority for representation, whether that representation arises from the law or the sole will of the taxpayer. In the latter case, the representative's authority must be documented in writing, either on paper or in digital documents duly signed by the grantor.

Thus, it is an obligation for anyone acting as a representative to prove the existence of their representation with the appropriate documentation, which must be in writing, either in physical or digital form. Additionally, in tax matters, only natural and legal persons can act as representatives, excluding groups without legal personality such as communities.

#### [Since when does a signed agreement apply?](#collapse12)

It applies from the same fiscal year as the signing of the agreement, and for the following four fiscal years, with the possibility of being extended or renewed if requested by the taxpayer.

#### [What are the contents of the arrangement agreement?](#collapse13)

a)  Place and date of formalisation.   
b)  Name and surnames or full business name and tax
identification number of the taxpayers referred to in the agreement.   
c)  Acceptance of the content of the agreement by the
taxpayer.   
d)  Description of the transactions referred to in the
agreement.   
e)  Indication of the essential information or circumstances
taken into account in the arrangement (“critical assumptions”)
and whose substantial modification would terminate the agreement.   
f)   Indication of the arm's length price, value,
or return accepted in the agreement, the transfer pricing methodology, and
financial indicator, among others.   
g)   Periods to which the agreement will apply
and its effective date.   
h)  Record of the taxpayer's authorisation to the Servicio de
Impuestos Internos for the publication of the criteria, economic, financial,
and commercial reasons, among others, and the methods under which the agreement
was established, if applicable.

#### [What are the legal guarantees that a taxpayer in Chile has with the entering of an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse14)

What taxpayers in Chile obtain from entering into an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) is legal certainty because once the APA, its extension, or renewal, as appropriate, becomes effective, the Servicio de Impuestos Internos cannot settle taxpayers who have participated in such agreements for tax differences in transfer pricing transactions within it. This is provided that the prices, values, or returns are those established or declared by the taxpayer in accordance with the terms outlined in the APA.

#### [Are the arrangement agreements and information provided protected by the discretion or duty of tax confidentiality contemplated in Article 35 of the Tax Code?](#collapse15)

Yes, the agreements of Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) and the information on which they were based are protected by the duty of tax confidentiality established by Article 35 of the Tax Code. Therefore, the officials of the SII cannot disclose the content of the respective agreement.

#### [Is there any particular incentive in the national regulation for taxpayers to submit an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse16)

Article 41 E of the Income Tax Law establishes that taxpayers who authorise the SII to publish the criteria, economic, financial, and commercial reasons, among others, and the methods by which the advance pricing arrangements were established, with their authorisation recorded in the respective agreement, will be included, if they authorise it, in a public list of socially responsible taxpayers.

The inclusion in this list will continue as long as the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) remains valid.

#### [What decision can the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, make regarding the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse17)

a)  They can fully accept the
Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) proposal.   
b)  They can partially accept
the APA proposal.   
c)  They can, at their exclusive
judgment, reject the advance pricing arrangement request through a resolution
or the application of the silent rejection established in Article 41 E of
the Income Tax Law.

#### [Does any administrative or legal recourse apply regarding the resolution rejecting an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse18)

No, the resolution rejecting an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) is not claimable or subject to any other recourse.

#### [How are the Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) materialised?](#collapse19)

If the SII fully or partially accepts the taxpayer's request, the advance pricing arrangement will be documented in an agreement, which will be signed by both the Servicio de Impuestos Internos, the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, and a representative of the taxpayer, expressly authorised for this purpose. It must include the information on which it is based.

#### [What is a prior consultation or pre-filing?](#collapse19-2)

Taxpayers interested in subscribing an Advance Transfer Pricing Arrangement
(APA) have the option of submitting a prior consultation or pre-filing, which
consists of a series of communications with the Service to evaluate the feasibility
of the arrangement. The content of the presentation of the prior consultation
is as follows:

* Identification of the persons or entities that are to carry out the transactions.
* Description of the operations subject to the advance arrangement.
* Basic elements of the valuation proposal you intend to formulate.
* Indication of an e-mail address for communications between the taxpayer and the Service.

The service, after an analysis of the submitted background, will communicate
the possibility of the presentation of the anticipated arrangement within two
months from the date of the presentation.

#### [What is a roll-back APA?](#collapse19-4)

When an Advance Transfer Pricing Arrangement (APA) is signed, an evaluation of the effects of the transactions carried out may be made, and if considered plausible, it may be extended up to a maximum of three business years prior to the signing of the APA.

[##### Filing application](#)

#### [Who can request an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse20)

An Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) can be requested by taxpayers resident or domiciled in Chile who carry out cross-border transactions with related parties abroad, as stipulated in Article 41 E of the Income Tax Law, for all or part of their related transactions, as determined by the taxpayer.

#### [How is an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request submitted?](#collapse21)

Taxpayers engaged in transactions with related parties, for the purposes outlined
in Article 41 E No. 7 of the Income Tax Law, must submit a request including
a description of the respective transactions, their arm's length prices, values,
or returns, and the period that the respective advance pricing arrangement
should cover, along with the information specified in Exempt Resolution No.
28 of 2025.

Additionally, the taxpayer must expressly indicate in the request whether
they authorize the SII to publish the criteria, economic, financial, and commercial
reasons, among others, and the methods that will serve as a basis for a potential
agreement.

To submit the request, the taxpayer must access the website [www.sii.cl](http://www.sii.cl/),
through the Online Services menu, selecting the option Income Statement / Advance
Pricing Arrangements (APA), enter their RUT (Tax Identification Number), and
authenticate themselves using their clave tributaria, clave única or any
other authentication mechanisms that may be implemented in the future. Prior
to this, the user must request enrolment to access the system and attach the
necessary documentation to resolve the request.

#### [What does the enrolment process entail in the procedure for submitting an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse22)

The SII, through Exempt Resolution 28 of 2025, has established a special enrolment
method for taxpayers to submit APA requests. It is a system accessible on
the website [www.sii.cl](http://www.sii.cl/), through the
Online Services menu, selecting the option Income Statement / Advance Pricing
Arrangements (APA). Through this system, enterprises must provide all the
required information specified in Exempt Resolution 28 of 2025, as well as
any additional information deemed necessary for the submission. They can also
consult the status of their documentation and attach any information that
may be requested by the SII during the procedure.

It is important to note that this enrolment process is distinct from the one
related to the request for electronic notifications specified in the first
section of Article 11 of the Tax Code.

Any notifications issued by the Servicio de Impuestos Internos within the APA
procedure follow the general rules stipulated in Articles 11 to 15 of the Tax
Code.

#### [How is the enrolment request conducted?](#collapse23)

To request enrolment, the taxpayer should follow these steps:

* Send an email to the address area.apa@sii.cl with
  the subject “Enrolment Request”.
* The email should identify the taxpayer requesting enrolment, with their business
  name and RUT (Tax Identification Number).
* If the enrolment request is being made by a representative of the taxpayer,
  the mandate or power that authorizes them to make this submission must be
  attached, whether it is a public or private instrument. For natural persons,
  a copy of their identification card must be attached.
* Subsequently, the taxpayer will receive an email from the SII informing their
  enrolment. Enabling them to access the system using the taxpayer's RUT and
  clave tributaria.

The APA will be considered submitted once the taxpayer uploads their request
and the minimum required information outlined in Exempt Resolution 28 of 2025
to the provided system.

#### [How are Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) requested in cases involving other foreign authorities?](#collapse24)

To involve foreign tax authorities in an APA, the taxpayer must explicitly require it in their request.

In this situation, the taxpayer must provide all documentation supporting the request, as well as any information that may have been provided to the foreign tax authority(es) within the framework of the APA request.

Once all the specified documentation is received, the Servicio de Impuestos Internos will analyse the proposal and its merits. If deemed appropriate, the SII will contact the respective foreign tax administrations to arrange meetings and discuss the advance pricing arrangement request.

If applicable, the total or partial acceptance of the request will lead to the signing of an agreement. The SII will then inform the corresponding tax administrations about the signing of the agreement.

#### [When can an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request be submitted?](#collapse25)

Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) requests can be submitted whenever the taxpayer deems it convenient.

#### [Is any fee or tax required to be paid in Chile for the submission of an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse26)

No, in Chile no fee or tax must be paid to submit an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request.

#### [What documentation must be attached to the formal request of an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse27)

The following information must be submitted:   
  
a)  Identification of the related
persons or entities who will engage in the transactions referred to in the
respective request.   
b)  Specification of the period that
the agreement should cover.   
c)  Corporate structure of the business
group to which the enterprise belongs, including parent companies, controlling
entities, subsidiaries, agencies, or directly or indirectly related parties
abroad.   
d)  Eight-column General Balance Sheet
at the sub-account level, on numbered paper, stamped by the Servicio de Impuestos
Internos and signed by the Legal Representative of the entity requesting the
arrangement, covering the two fiscal years preceding the submission of the
request.   
e)  Audited financial statements and
their explanatory notes from the entity requiring the arrangement, if issued
and audited in due time, for the two fiscal years preceding the submission
of the request.   
f)  Copies of contracts for transactions
with related parties abroad that are relevant to the arrangement request,
along with a simple translation into Spanish if applicable. These contracts
may concern the distribution, production, use and licensing of intangible
assets, financial transactions, and service provision, among others.   
g)  Identification of the holders
of intellectual property rights such as patents, trademarks, or other intangible
assets that directly or indirectly affect the transactions related to the
advance pricing arrangement request.   
h)      Information regarding any other advance
pricing arrangements or mutual agreement procedures entered into or in progress
concerning entities within the group that directly or indirectly relate to
the transactions specified in the advance pricing arrangement request.   
i)  Must include a transfer pricing
study.   
j)  Any other information that the taxpayer
considers relevant for the analysis of the transactions included in the submitted
request.

#### [What should be the content of the transfer pricing report or study that must accompany the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse28)

The content of the transfer pricing report or study is set forth in SII Exempt
Resolution 28 of 2025.

1. A detailed description of the transactions subject to the
   request, the functions or
   activities performed, the assets, and the risks assumed by
   the various related parties involved in the transactions of
   the agreement.
2. A description of the main
   activities conducted by the
   companies within that group,
   including the location(s) where these activities take place.
3. A description and justification
   of the economic assumptions
   and conditions of the proposal,
   indicating aspects such as
   estimated sales volumes, market conditions, terms of international
   exchange, exchange rates, and any other essential circumstances
   or information for the proposal.
4. A description of the group's
   transfer pricing policy, including
   the method or methods for
   setting prices adopted
   by the group, justifying their
   alignment with the arm's length principle.
5. Explanation regarding the
   selection of the transfer
   pricing method chosen in the
   proposal and the profitability
   indicator, if applicable.
6. Criteria for selecting
   comparable transactions or
   enterprises, price, value,
   or return ranges of comparable
   transactions or enterprises,
   and comparability adjustments
   made, if applicable.
7. Specification of the value,
   price or profitability proposed
   for the transactions to be
   covered by the application.
8. If a segmented analysis
   is proposed—by product, activity, country,
   or by transactions with related and independent parties—the
   report must explain how such segmentation was carried out,
   indicating the accounting accounts used and the allocation
   method.
9. Where the proposal involves
   a transfer of functions, assets
   or risks between related parties,
   the commercial or economic
   benefits that the Chilean
   entity will derive from the
   restructuring must be specified.
10. Any other aspect that
    the taxpayer deems relevant
    for the analysis of the transactions
    included in the arrangement.

#### [Can a taxpayer withdraw its Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse29)

Yes, according to Article 42 of Law 19,880 of May 2003, any interested party can withdraw its request when it is not prohibited by the legal system, or renounce its rights, provided that this is done before the signing of the arrangement agreement or it is rejected by the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable.

To materialise the withdrawal, the taxpayer must send an email to the address area.apa@sii.cl, including a document signed by those who have express authority to withdraw the request, copies of the powers that state their authority, and the corresponding identification cards.

In these cases, and in accordance with section 3 of Article 14 of Law 19,880, the SII will issue the corresponding resolution, recognising the withdrawal of the submission.

[##### Filing process dealines](#)

#### [What period do the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, have to respond to an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse30)

The Service(s) has(have) a period of 6 months to respond regarding the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA), counted from when the taxpayer has provided or made available to the Service(s) the totality of the information necessary to resolve it.

#### [Since when are the 12 months counted for the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, to respond to an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request?](#collapse31)

The period begins counting from the submission or availability of all the information requested by the Service(s). In the case of an Advance Pricing Arrangement unrelated to the import of goods, the period starts from the notification of the certificate issued by the Head of the Departamento de Análisis de Riesgos Internacionales, under the Subdirección de Fiscalización of the Servicio de Impuestos Internos. For an APA concerning the import of goods, where the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas is also involved, both Services must issue a certificate. However, the 12-month period is always counted from the certification issued by the Servicio de Impuestos Internos, as per the fourth resolved of Resolution No. 54 of 2016.

#### [During the review of the taxpayer's Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request, can the Servicio de Impuestos Internos make an alternative proposal?](#collapse32)

Yes, the SII can propose to the interested party, before the 12-month period it has to respond, an alternative proposal regarding the transactions subject to the request.

#### [What happens if the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, do not respond within the legal 12-month period?](#collapse33)

If the Service(s) fail to respond within the specified timeframe, the taxpayer's request is deemed rejected, enabling the latter to submit a new Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) request.

[##### Process completion mechanisms](#)

#### [Can Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) be renewed or extended?](#collapse34)

Yes, an APA can be extended or renewed through a prior written agreement signed
by the requestor, the Servicio de Impuestos Internos, and the Servicio Nacional
de Aduanas, when applicable. For this purpose, the requestor must submit a written
extension request sent by email to [area.apa@sii.cl](mailto:area.apa@sii.cl),
addressed to the Head of the Departamento de Análisis de Riesgos Internacionales,
under the Subdirección de Fiscalización, at least 6 months before
the expiration of the originally signed agreement.

#### [What is the period in which Advance Pricing Arrangements (APA) can be extended or renewed?](#collapse35)

The validity period of an extended or renewed APA may be extended for up to the five subsequent fiscal years following the expiration of the previously executed APA; provided, however, that the Service, in the exercise of its powers, may authorize a shorter term, in which case the period of validity covered by such extension or renewal shall be specified in the corresponding agreement record.

#### [Can the taxpayer resign from an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse36)

Yes, the taxpayer can resign from an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) if
there have been substantial changes in the essential information or circumstances
considered at the time of its entering, extension, or renewal. To do so, the
taxpayer must express its intention by sending an email to [area.apa@sii.cl](mailto:area.apa@sii.cl),
addressed to the Head of the Departamento de Análisis de Riesgos Internacionales,
under the Subdirección de Fiscalización.

The taxpayer's notice must include a detailed explanation of the changes in the
essential information or circumstances referred to in the preceding paragraph,
that justify the nullification of the advance pricing arrangement, its extension,
or renewal. The Servicio de Impuestos Internos will communicate this situation,
when applicable, to the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas and the other foreign tax
administrations involved in the arrangement.

#### [In the event of resigning from an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA), when does the Agreement become ineffective?](#collapse37)

The Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) becomes ineffective from the date of the taxpayer's notice submission, allowing the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable, to exercise all the faculties granted by the law concerning the taxpayer's transactions.

Notwithstanding the aforementioned, the taxpayer retains the right to submit a new APA request, providing the necessary information to justify it.

#### [Can a signed arrangement agreement be revoked?](#collapse38)

The Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when
applicable, can revoke the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA), as well as its
extension or renewal, at any time. This is done through the issuance and notification
of a resolution when they determine the existence of any of the following
circumstances, under which the APA request was accepted:

1. The taxpayer's request was based on erroneous information.
2. The taxpayer's request was based on maliciously false information.
3. The essential information or circumstances considered at the time of its
   entering, extension, or renewal have substantially changed.

In APAs concerning the import of goods, the revocation resolution must be
signed by the Director of the Servicio de Impuestos Internos and the Director
of the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas.

#### [What are the effects of revoking an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse39)

As a result of revoking an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA), the Servicio
de Impuestos Internos and the Servicio Nacional de Aduanas, when applicable,
may issue the corresponding tax settlement or tax bills, readjustments, interests,
and fines, for which a revocation resolution will be issued. This resolution
takes effect as follows:  

1. When the request was based on erroneous information or a substantial change
   in the essential information or circumstances considered at the time of its
   entering, extension, or renewal, the resolution will take effect upon its
   notification to the taxpayer.
2. When the revocation is based on the maliciously
   false nature of the information provided in the request, extension, or renewal,
   the advance pricing arrangement will be ineffective from the date of entering
   the original agreement or its renewals or extensions, when such information
   was invoked by the taxpayer.

#### [Does any recourse apply to the resolution revoking an Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA)?](#collapse40)

No, the resolution revoking the Advance Pricing Arrangement (APA) is not claimable and no recourse will apply in its regard.

#### [What sanctions can be applied for the submission of maliciously false information?](#collapse41)

The submission of maliciously false information in a request for an Advance
Pricing Arrangement (APA) that has been totally or partially accepted by the
Servicio de Impuestos Internos will be sanctioned with a fine from 50% to 300%
of the value of the evaded tax, and ordinary imprisonment in its medium (541
days to 3 years) to maximum term (3 years and one day to 5 years), in accordance
with the first paragraph of No. 4 of Article 97 of the Tax Code.
